How to choose

Slip lead or slip collar?

A slip lead is collar and lead in one piece, so it's quick to put on and take off. A slip collar stays on the dog and clips to whatever lead you already own. If you want one tool for walks and car trips, get the lead. If you already have a lead you love, get the collar.

4ft or 6ft slip lead?

4ft keeps your dog close, which suits busy footpaths and vet waiting rooms. 6ft gives more room on a normal walk and for training.

5m or 10m long line?

Start with 5m. It's easier to handle while your dog learns to come back. Go to 10m once recall is solid and you want to give more freedom at the park or beach.

Rope or Biothane?

Rope is soft and grippy. Biothane is waterproof, doesn't smell and wipes clean. If you're often at the beach or in the rain, go Biothane.

How do I size a slip collar?

Measure around the widest part of your dog's head and add 2–3cm.
